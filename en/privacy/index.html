At a glance

  • Your CAD files are opened only on your device; they are never sent to us or to any other server.
  • Voice commands are transcribed on your device; audio is neither stored nor sent.
  • No account is required. There is no advertising, usage analytics or tracking.
  • For subscriptions, we process purchase information, a random user ID and basic device and connection data. Your payment details (card, etc.) never reach us; payments are handled by Apple or Google.

2. Data we do not process

  • Your files: The CAD files you open and their contents (models, measurements, notes), and the files you save in the app such as PDF, DXF, CSV and images, are processed and stored only on your device. If device backup is turned on, the operating system may include the app's data in your iCloud or Google backup; we have no access to these backups. If you open a file from a cloud location such as iCloud Drive or Google Drive, that file is subject to the terms of that service.
  • Audio: The microphone is on only while you hold down the voice command button and turns off when you release it. Speech is transcribed by the operating system's on-device recognizer; audio is not recorded and is not sent to us, Apple or Google.
  • We do not access your location, contacts, photos, advertising identifier or similar data. The app contains no advertising, usage analytics (which features you use and how much) or crash-reporting tools.

3. Data we process and why

3.1 Subscription

To verify your subscription and unlock the app, the following data is processed:

  • App user ID: A random identifier created when the app is first opened; it is not linked to your name or email address.
  • Purchase information: The selected plan, purchase and renewal dates, trial and cancellation status, price and currency, and the store's transaction ID.
  • Technical information: Device model, operating system and app version, language and region settings, and your IP address during the connection.

This data is used to verify subscriptions, restore purchases, prevent abuse, and produce aggregated (non-identifying) revenue reports.

How it is collected: Electronically and automatically, through the app and the store, when the app checks your subscription status or when you make a purchase.

Legal basis: Performance of a contract for verifying subscriptions and restoring purchases (KVKK Art. 5/2-c; GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)); our legitimate interests for preventing abuse and producing aggregated revenue reports (KVKK Art. 5/2-f; GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)), namely protecting the service against misuse and managing our business.

Is it required? This data is necessary to provide the subscription. Without it, the subscription cannot be verified and the app shows only the subscription screen.

We do not profile you. Whether the app unlocks is decided automatically, based only on your subscription status in the store; if you think something is wrong, contact us.

3.2 Payment

Payments are handled by Apple on the App Store and by Google on Google Play. Your card and billing details are subject to their privacy policies and are not shared with us.

3.3 Support

If you email us, your email address and message are processed only to respond to you. They are collected electronically through the email you send. Legal basis: performance of a contract and our legitimate interests (KVKK Art. 5/2-c and 5/2-f; GDPR Art. 6(1)(b) and 6(1)(f)).

3.4 On-device log file

To help solve problems, the app keeps a technical log file on your device. The log also contains the names of the files you open and save. It is not sent to us and is deleted when you delete the app.

3.5 This website

This website uses no cookies, analytics or advertising, and loads no fonts or scripts from other sites. Your light or dark theme preference is stored only in your browser and is not sent to us.

As with any website, the server of the hosting provider that publishes this site records your IP address, the requested page, the date and time, and browser information in its access logs during your visit. These logs are used only to operate and secure the site. Legal basis: our legitimate interests (KVKK Art. 5/2-f; GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).

4. Recipients

  • Apple Inc. (App Store) and Google LLC (Google Play): purchases and payments.
  • RevenueCat, Inc. (USA): subscription verification and management. RevenueCat processes the data in section 3.1 on our behalf and under our instructions.
  • GitHub, Inc. (USA): hosting this website. The access logs in section 3.5 are transferred abroad.
  • Our email hosting provider in Türkiye: storing support emails.

RevenueCat's servers are located in the United States, so the data in section 3.1 is transferred abroad. We do not sell your data or share it with anyone for advertising.

5. Retention

Subscription records are kept for the duration of your subscription and afterwards for as long as required by law; they are then deleted or anonymized. Support emails are kept for up to 2 years after the matter is closed. Deleting the app does not delete subscription records.

Deleting the app removes its data from your device, such as settings and the log file. The files you save:

  • On iPhone and iPad are kept in the ÇİZEN Viewer Max folder in the Files app and are deleted together with the app. Files you move to another folder remain.
  • On Android are saved to the Documents/ÇİZEN Viewer Max folder and remain after you delete the app; you can delete them yourself.

6. App permissions

  • Microphone and speech recognition: Used only for voice commands and only when you ask. The rest of the app works without this permission.
  • Internet: Used only to verify your subscription.

7. Your rights

Under Article 11 of the KVKK, you have the right to learn whether your personal data is processed and request information about it; to learn the purpose of processing and whether it is used accordingly; to know the third parties in Türkiye or abroad to whom it is transferred; to request correction of incomplete or inaccurate data, and erasure or destruction where the legal conditions are met, and to have these actions notified to the third parties to whom the data was transferred; to object to a result against you arising solely from automated analysis; and to claim compensation for damage caused by unlawful processing.

If you are in the European Union, you also have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, objection and data portability under the GDPR, and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

Send your requests to bilgi@mubitek.com or to the postal address above. We respond free of charge within 30 days. To help us find your record, please tell us the store you purchased from and, if possible, the order number on the store's purchase receipt.

If your request is rejected, you find our answer insufficient or we do not answer in time, you may lodge a complaint with the Turkish Personal Data Protection Board (Kişisel Verileri Koruma Kurulu) within 30 days of learning our answer, and in any case within 60 days of your request (KVKK Art. 14).

8. Children

ÇİZEN Viewer Max is a professional app made for people working in engineering and manufacturing; it is not directed to children. The age rating shown in the stores (e.g. 4+) only means that the app contains no violence, inappropriate language or similar content; it does not mean that the app is made for children.

We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. The app does not ask anyone for a name, email address or similar information, and subscriptions are purchased through an Apple or Google account. If you believe a child under 13 has sent us personal data (for example by email), contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

Connections used for subscription verification are encrypted (HTTPS). Because your files never leave your device, their security depends on the protection of your device.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The current version is always published on this page with its “Last updated” date. We announce material changes in the app or on the store page.
